# --- B2: Gestión conversacional avanzada ---
B2 | Nuance           | An interviewer asks a layered, complex question that requires thought. | That is a nuanced question — there are a few different ways to look at it.
B2 | Nuance           | You want to separate two related but distinct concepts in your answer. | I think we need to distinguish between two different aspects of this question.
B2 | Nuance           | You mostly agree but want to add an important condition to your agreement. | I am inclined to agree, though I would add the caveat that context matters a lot here.
B2 | Nuance           | You need more information before you can commit to an answer. | Before I commit to a position on that, I would want to understand more about the details.
B2 | Clarity          | You want to make sure you are addressing the real question being asked. | I want to make sure I am addressing the core of what you are actually asking me.
B2 | Clarity          | You said something complicated and want to restate it more clearly. | To put it differently, what I am trying to convey is that the approach needs to evolve.
B2 | Clarity          | You want to frame your answer within the broader context of the situation. | If I understand the broader context correctly, then the key issue is alignment between teams.
B2 | Thinking         | You need time to formulate a thoughtful, well-structured answer. | Let me think out loud for a moment — I want to give you a well-considered answer.
B2 | Engagement       | Something the interviewer said resonated with your own experience. | That resonates with me — particularly the point you made about long-term growth.
B2 | Engagement       | You appreciate a challenging question being raised in the conversation. | I appreciate you raising that — it is something I have been reflecting on as well.
B2 | Engagement       | You want to highlight the most significant aspect of the situation. | What strikes me as most significant about this situation is the opportunity it presents.
B2 | Pushback         | You want to respectfully disagree with a point someone just made. | I would push back slightly on that — my experience suggests a different outcome.
