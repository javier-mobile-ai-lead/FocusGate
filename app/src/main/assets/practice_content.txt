# CONV: MOBILE_DEV | Talking about app architecture
APP: Javier, the new developer is asking about our app architecture. Can you explain it?
YOU: Sure. We use the MVVM pattern with Clean Architecture. This separates the UI logic from the business logic.
APP: Can you explain what MVVM means in simple terms?
YOU: MVVM stands for Model View ViewModel. The Model handles data, the ViewModel processes it, and the View just displays what the ViewModel tells it.
APP: And how does Clean Architecture fit into that?
YOU: Clean Architecture adds layers. We have the presentation layer, the domain layer with business rules, and the data layer for APIs and the database.
APP: Why did the team choose this architecture?
YOU: It makes the code easier to test and maintain. Each layer only knows about the layer below it. This reduces dependencies.
APP: How long did it take to set up this architecture?
YOU: The initial setup took about a week. But it has saved us a lot of time when we need to make changes or add features.

YOU: I prefer working from home most of the time. I am more productive and save commute time.
APP: Do you not miss the social interaction with colleagues?
YOU: Yes sometimes I miss seeing people in person. That is the main downside of remote work.
APP: How do you stay connected with your team when working remotely?
YOU: We have daily standups on video call and we use Slack for everything else. It works well.
APP: Would you ever consider going back to an office full time?
YOU: Maybe one or two days a week for collaboration. But I would not want to go back full time.

# CONV: SMALL_TALK | Talking about a recent trip
APP: You mentioned you traveled recently. Where did you go?
YOU: I went to Cusco for five days. It is one of the most beautiful places in Peru.
APP: Did you visit Machu Picchu?
YOU: Yes that was the highlight. I woke up at four in the morning to see the sunrise there.
APP: Was it as impressive as the photos?
YOU: Even more impressive. The photos cannot capture the scale and the feeling of being there.
APP: How was the altitude? I heard it can make you feel sick.
YOU: The first day was difficult. I had a headache and felt tired. But by the second day I was fine.

# CONV: SMALL_TALK | Discussing work-life balance
APP: Do you think it is easy to have a good balance between work and personal life?
YOU: It can be difficult, especially when you work from home. The boundaries get blurry.
APP: How do you separate work time from personal time?
YOU: I have a fixed end time. At six I close my laptop and do not check work messages until the next day.
APP: That is very disciplined. Do you sometimes work on weekends?
YOU: Only if there is an urgent deadline. But I try to protect my weekends as much as possible.
APP: What do you do when you feel burned out?
YOU: I take a short break, go for a walk, or do something creative that has nothing to do with technology.
